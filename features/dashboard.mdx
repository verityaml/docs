The dashboard is the single view that answers the question every BSA officer asks: *"How ready are we?"*

## Bank-readiness score

At the top of the dashboard, a **Bank-Readiness Score** shows a real-time percentage measuring how prepared your compliance program is for an examination. This isn't a static number someone entered — it's computed from the evidence status of every criterion across every regulatory domain.

## Domain cards

Below the score, **domain cards** break down readiness by compliance area. For a BSA/AML program, that's the five FFIEC pillars:

- Internal Controls
- Independent Testing
- BSA Officer Oversight
- Training
- CDD/KYC

Each card shows its own score and a count of how many criteria are sufficient, partial, or missing.

If you see a domain card with gaps — say CDD/KYC at 60% with two missing criteria — that's the system telling you where to focus *before* the examination letter arrives.

## Priority gaps

The **Priority Gaps panel** lists the highest-priority unfilled criteria across all domains, ranked by regulatory importance. This is your team's to-do list, derived directly from the regulatory requirements.

## Multiple programs

Verity supports multiple compliance programs. Click the tab at the top of the dashboard to switch between them:

| Program | Framework | Domains |
|---------|-----------|---------|
| BSA/AML | FFIEC BSA/AML Examination Manual | 5 pillars |
| Third-Party Oversight | OCC 2023-17 | 8 monitoring areas |

When you switch programs, the entire dashboard reconfigures — different domains, different criteria, different score. Same platform, different regulatory framework.
