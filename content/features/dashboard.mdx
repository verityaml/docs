The dashboard is the single view that answers the question every BSA officer asks: *"How ready are we?"*

## Bank-readiness score

At the top of the dashboard, a **Bank-Readiness Score** shows a real-time measure of how prepared your compliance program is for an examination. This isn't a static number someone entered — it's computed from the novelty-weighted evidence scores of every criterion across every regulatory domain. Scores are unbounded integers that reward diverse evidence rather than duplicate documentation.

## Domain cards

Below the score, **domain cards** break down readiness by compliance area. For a BSA/AML program, that's the five FFIEC pillars:

- Internal Controls
- Independent Testing
- BSA Officer Oversight
- Training
- CDD/KYC

Each card shows its own score, a 30-day sparkline showing recent score movement (with historical v1 percentage scores rendered as a dashed clay line), and a count of how many criteria are sufficient, partial, or missing. Domains with a configured readiness threshold show a **progress bar** that fills relative to the target — 100% means the domain meets its threshold. Three color bands indicate status: forest (at or above target), clay (approaching), red (well below). An amber **▲** glyph appears when the score is below target. Admins can set or adjust these thresholds via the slider on each domain's detail page.

If you see a domain card with gaps — say CDD/KYC at 60% with two missing criteria — that's the system telling you where to focus *before* the examination letter arrives.

## Run Gap Analysis

The **Run Gap Analysis** button is always available between the readiness score and the domain grid. It populates scoring criteria (if not already present) and then matches evidence from all your examinations at once — a one-click way to refresh your entire program's evidence coverage.

## Quick Links sidebar

The **Quick Links** sidebar on the right gives you one-click access to Active Obligations, the Response Archive, the Reference Library, and Settings. A **New Obligation** button here lets you start a new examination or consent order immediately. The **Export Report** link opens the readiness report preview for the active program.

## Priority gaps

The **Priority Gaps** panel in the sidebar lists the highest-priority unfilled criteria across all domains, ranked by regulatory importance. This is your team's to-do list, derived directly from the regulatory requirements.

## Evidence count badges

Each domain card shows an evidence count alongside the gap count — e.g., "12 evidence" — computed from linked evidence across all scoring criteria in that domain.

## Score snapshots

The **Snapshots** section below Priority Gaps lets you capture point-in-time records of your program's scores. Click **Save Snapshot**, optionally add a label like "Partnership Baseline" or "Pre-60-Day Review", and confirm.

Click any snapshot row to expand its domain breakdown inline — per-domain scores, sufficient/total criteria, and missing counts. When you have two or more snapshots, expand **Compare Snapshots** to see a side-by-side diff table with per-domain score deltas color-coded green for improvement and red for regression.

<Tip>
Snapshots are the primary tool for review presentations: open the dashboard, scroll to snapshots, select the baseline and the latest, and present the improvement.
</Tip>

## Multiple programs

Verity supports multiple compliance programs. Click the tab at the top of the dashboard to switch between them:

| Program | Framework | Domains |
|---------|-----------|---------|
| BSA/AML | FFIEC BSA/AML Examination Manual | 5 pillars |
| Third-Party Oversight | OCC 2023-17 | 8 monitoring areas |

When you switch programs, the entire dashboard reconfigures — different domains, different criteria, different score. Same platform, different regulatory framework.
