## Add member directly

<ParamField path="method" type="POST">`/api/org/members/add-direct`</ParamField>

Adds a new member to the organization directly (admin-only). Creates a user account with a random password and sends a password reset email.

<ParamField body="name" type="string" required>
  Display name for the new member.
</ParamField>

<ParamField body="email" type="string" required>
  Email address for the new member.
</ParamField>

<ParamField body="role" type="string">
  Member role. One of: `admin`, `member`. Defaults to `member`.
</ParamField>

<ResponseField name="data.userId" type="string">
  The created user's ID.
</ResponseField>

<ResponseField name="data.email" type="string">
  The member's email address.
</ResponseField>

<ResponseField name="data.role" type="string">
  The assigned role.
</ResponseField>

<Note>
This endpoint logs an `member.added_direct` audit event.
</Note>

## Send setup email

<ParamField path="method" type="POST">`/api/org/members/send-setup-email`</ParamField>

Sends a password reset email to a member who was added directly, allowing them to set their own password.

---

## Resend invitation

<ParamField path="method" type="POST">`/api/org/invitations/resend`</ParamField>

Resends a pending invitation email to a member who hasn't accepted yet.

## Roles

| Role | Permissions |
|------|------------|
| `admin` | Full access — manage settings, team members, all examinations and programs |
| `member` | View and work on examinations and compliance programs |

## Organization scope

The organization is the tenant boundary. All data — examinations, compliance programs, evidence, remediation items — is scoped to the organization. Users can belong to one organization.

### Audit log

All significant actions are logged to an immutable, org-scoped audit log. Events include:
- Member additions and removals
- Examination creation, archival, and deletion
- Status changes on examinations and remediation items
- Evidence uploads and deletions
